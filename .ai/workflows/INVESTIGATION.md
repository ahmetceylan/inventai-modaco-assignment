# Investigation Workflow

Before writing code:

## Step 1

Identify every affected file.

## Step 2

Explain the current implementation.

## Step 3

Explain the expected behavior.

## Step 4

Identify the root cause.

## Step 5

Explain why the bug exists.

## Step 6

Evaluate:

- Security
- Performance
- Reliability
- Developer Experience

## Step 7

List possible solutions.

## Step 8

Recommend the smallest safe fix.

Stop here.

Wait before implementing.
